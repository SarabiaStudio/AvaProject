02 My Body — ReflecTea Shelf Furniture 2024
02

ReflecTea Shelf

I designed a modular, transportable piece of furniture that transforms any corner into a reading and tea space. I built a 1:1 prototype in cardboard with three simultaneous uses: shelf, seat and table. It was a group project that allowed me to deepen my understanding of spatially efficient, user-centred design.

03 Maison à Bordeaux Scale Model 2024
03

Maison à Bordeaux

I built a 1:25 scale model of Rem Koolhaas's work, in groups where each team tackled a different floor. It was my first experience in architectural model-making: I learned about technical precision, attention to detail and collaboration as a methodology of spatial exploration.

Architectural Model

04 Distortia — CCCB Installation 2025
04

Distortia

I proposed an intervention in the CCCB courtyard in Barcelona: a mirror labyrinth that reflects visitors fragmented and distorted. When they exit, they remain the same. It's a metaphor for how experiences alter our perception but not our core identity. I researched types of reflective surfaces: concave, convex, warped, multi-angle and kinetic aluminium.

Installation / Space
CCCB, Barcelona
Mirrors / Steel
05 Aeyde Office Interiors 2025
05

Aeyde Office

I redesigned the open space at IED Barcelona as an office for Aeyde, a Berlin-based footwear brand. My concept was "Transparency": the manufacturing process visible, not hidden. I used glass brick, steel and chrome to create an open, reflective atmosphere where showroom, workshop, workspace and meeting room coexist with materials that celebrate craft.

Interior Design
Aeyde, Berlin
AutoCAD · Rhino
06 Reflecta — Sistine Chapel Conceptual 2026
06

Reflecta

I developed this project in pairs during my Erasmus in Rome. We proposed a redesign of the conclave spaces in the Sistine Chapel: triangular cabins with mirrored geometry that fragment and reflect the surrounding frescoes. For me, it's architecture as a dialogue between the sacred and the contemporary. The material palette includes reflective glass, dark oak, lime walls, terracotta and aged brass.

Conceptual Design
Sistine Chapel, Rome
