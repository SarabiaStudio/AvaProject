02 My Body — ReflecTea Shelf Furniture 2024
02

ReflecTea Shelf

I designed a modular, portable piece of furniture that transforms any corner into a reading and tea space. I built a 1:1 prototype in cardboard with three simultaneous uses: shelving unit, seat and table. 03 Maison à Bordeaux Model 2024
03

Maison à Bordeaux

I built a 1:25 scale model of Rem Koolhaas's iconic house, working in groups where each team tackled a different floor. It was my first experience in architectural model-making: I learned about technical precision, attention to detail, and collaboration as a methodology for spatial exploration.

04 Distortia — CCCB Installation 2025
04

Distortia

I proposed an intervention in the courtyard of the CCCB in Barcelona: a labyrinth of mirrors that reflects visitors fragmented and distorted. When they exit, they remain unchanged — a metaphor for how experiences alter our perception but not our core identity. I researched typologies of reflective surfaces: concave, convex, warped, multi-angle and kinetic aluminium.

Installation / Space
CCCB, Barcelona
Mirrors / Steel
05 Aeyde Office Interior 2025
05

Aeyde Office

I redesigned the open space at IED Barcelona as an office for Aeyde, a Berlin-based footwear brand. My concept was "Transparency": the manufacturing process visible, not hidden. I used glass brick, steel and chrome to create an open, reflective environment where showroom, workshop, workstations and meeting room coexist with materials that celebrate craftsmanship.

Interior Design
Aeyde, Berlin
AutoCAD · Rhino
06 Reflecta — Sistine Chapel Conceptual 2026
06

Reflecta

I developed this project with a partner during my Erasmus in Rome. We proposed a redesign of the conclave spaces inside the Sistine Chapel: triangular mirror-geometry cabins that fragment and reflect the surrounding frescoes. For me, it is architecture as a dialogue between the sacred and the contemporary. The material palette includes reflective glass, dark oak, lime-washed walls, terracotta and aged brass.

Conceptual Design
Sistine Chapel, Rome
